Privacy Policy for Calendar Islam

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Calendar Islam respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Calendar Islam app.

Information We Collect

Calendar Islam does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Location Information

The app may use your device's location to provide location-based features such as prayer times. Location information is used only for the functionality provided by the app and is not shared with other users.

Internet Access

The app may connect to the internet to retrieve or update certain information and services.

Data Sharing

Calendar Islam does not sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Advertising

Calendar Islam does not display advertisements.

Children's Privacy

Calendar Islam is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer through the contact information provided on Google Play.
